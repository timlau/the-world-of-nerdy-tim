There's a new corner of the Omarchy world worth knowing: [Omatools](https://www.omatools.org/), eight creative apps built for the desktop instead of merely ported to it. They follow your Omarchy theme, keep your work local, and come with short video guides. Here's what's in the box.

## The lineup

- **[OmaBroadcast](https://www.omatools.org/#omabroadcast)** — record and stream live video. Build a studio from screens, windows, cameras, your phone, media and slides; switch scenes while recording to MKV/MP4/MOV or streaming to multiple destinations, with green screen, a sound mixer and Stream Deck control.

- **[OmaEdit](https://www.omatools.org/#omaedit)** — a keyboard-first video editor. Cut on a multi-track timeline, add transitions, titles and captions, grade with OpenColorIO and scopes, mix with effects and a loudness meter, then export via presets and a render queue.

- **[OmaRAW](https://www.omatools.org/#omaraw)** — a photo library and RAW editor. Import a shoot, rate and organize your keepers, then develop them with curves, colour wheels and local masks. Object removal and RAW denoise run on your own machine; exports include JPEG, TIFF, WebP, AVIF, JPEG XL and PSD.

- **[OmaShow](https://www.omatools.org/#omashow)** — make and present slide decks offline. It follows your theme, opens PowerPoint and Keynote files, supports builds, transitions and presenter view, and can export to PDF, images, video or PowerPoint — even from the command line with no window open.

- **[Omanta](https://www.omatools.org/#omanta)** — a file manager that feels like part of the desktop. It's a drop-in replacement for GNOME Files with the same keys: tabs, split view, undo, search inside files, archives, network shares and phones. One switch in the Omarchy menu flips between it and your current manager.

- **[Omamesh](https://www.omatools.org/#omamesh)** — turn photos into textured 3D models. Drop in a picture and get a `.glb` next to it, viewable with clay, normals or wireframe shading. Scale and orient it for printing, then export GLB, OBJ or STL for Blender, a game engine or a 3D printer. Nothing is uploaded.

- **[OmaFont](https://www.omatools.org/#omafont)** — a font manager that shows every installed family in its own typeface, using your own sample text. Drag in a file, folder or zip to preview and validate before installing; deactivate fonts, kill duplicates, build collections and send a monospace font to the terminal from the CLI too.

- **[OmaAudio](https://www.omatools.org/#omaaudio)** — record, edit, mix and deliver audio. Clean up voices, cut a podcast or score a film with multitrack editing, LV2/CLAP/VST3 effects, buses, sends and automation. It opens AAF and OMF timelines and hits loudness targets for delivery.

## Worth a look

Each app has an install page on GitHub and a guide on the site, so you can try one without committing to the whole suite. It's great to see this much serious creative software being made *for* Omarchy.

Start with [Omatools](https://www.omatools.org/) and see what catches your eye. 🎬📷🎛️
