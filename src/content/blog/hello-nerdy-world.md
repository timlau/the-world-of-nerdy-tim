Every few years I think about starting a blog. Then I get distracted by a package that won't
build, a window manager config that needs one more tweak, or a plugin that sounds *almost* right.
This time I actually finished the site, so here we are.

## Why bother?

Most of what I learn ends up in commit messages, chat threads, or a text file called
`notes-2026-10-whatever.md`. None of those are searchable when future me needs them. A blog is a
slightly more organised version of that text file, with RSS.

## What to expect

- Notes on Linux desktops and the endless Hyprland configuration rabbit hole
- Adventures in audio plugin packaging
- Small programs in Zig, Python, and whatever else seems fun
- The occasional vinyl record or synth detour

No posting schedule, no growth strategy, no newsletter popup. Just words on a page, mostly for my
own future reference.

If you want to follow along, the [RSS feed](../../rss.xml) is the least
annoying way.

Let's see how long this lasts. 🚀
