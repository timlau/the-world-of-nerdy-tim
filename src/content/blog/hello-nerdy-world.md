## Why bother?

Most of what I learn ends up in commit messages, chat threads, or a text file called
`notes-2026-10-whatever.md`. None of them are searchable when future me needs them. A blog is a
slightly more organized version of that text file, with RSS.

## What to expect

- Notes on Linux desktops
- Detours into whatever catches my interest:
  - Programming
  - Space exploration
  - Music creation
  - Movies
  - Rock and metal
  - Content creation
  - AI

No posting schedule, no growth strategy, no newsletter popup. Just words on a page, mostly for my
own future reference.

If you want to follow along, the [RSS feed](../../rss.xml) is the least annoying way to do that.

Let's see how long this lasts. 🚀
