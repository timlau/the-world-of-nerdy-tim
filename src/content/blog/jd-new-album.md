**New awesome album from one of the best danish rock band, Junkyard Drive.**


### Press Release from the band
After four explosive singles and two videos, Danish rock forerunners Junkyard Drive release the fifth album, ‘SQUEEZE’ today, November 6th 2026. The album consists of ten modern, energetic, and melodic rock songs that redefine the band’s sound without losing their characteristic expression, sky-high energy level, or accomplished songwriting.

‘SQUEEZE’ is the result of an uncompromising DIY approach, with the band taking charge of the entire process themselves, from the very first demo to the final release. The album was made possible thanks to enormous support from the band’s loyal fans, who funded the release through a crowdfunding campaign. 
Singer Kristian Johansen states in connection with the album release:
“We are super proud to release our new album, 'SQUEEZE'.
A huge amount of hard work has gone into this record, and at the same time, we have experienced massive support from our loyal fans. They supported the release through crowdfunding, and we honestly couldn't have done it without them.

The songs on the album are personal and completely honest. The sound is fresh and has a more modern and tight feel than what we usually do. If you're expecting a classic Junkyard Drive record, you'll probably be surprised. In a good way 😉

I hope people will listen to the album. We put everything we have into these 10 tracks.”
SQUEEZE’ is recorded in collaboration with producers Mads Mølgaard and Christian Afel at the former Farvemøllen in Copenhagen’s Nordvest district, with mastering by Chris Kreutzfeldt and artwork created by the band itself. The album is released on the band’s own label Drive Music and is available in three vinyl editions - 100 Orange/black splatter copies, 100 orange copies and 300 black copies.

In support of ‘SQUEEZE’ Junkyard Drive recently brought their high-powered show to Germany and UK for 12 dates, and two weeks ago they kicked off the Danish leg of the tour. The tour continues later this month with shows in the biggest Danish cities throughout March with 15 Danish dates, while dates abroad will be announced in the near future.
